# 💡 Key Learnings

One of the biggest lessons I learned today is that Large Language Models do not actually understand complete sentences like humans do.

Instead, they first convert text into smaller units called **tokens**, process those tokens, predict the next token, and gradually generate meaningful responses.

I also learned that the quality of an AI response depends heavily on the quality of the prompt given to the model.

---

# 🧠 Understanding Tokens

Today I learned that a token can represent:

- A complete word
- Part of a word
- A number
- A punctuation mark
- A special symbol

This helped me understand why the number of words and the number of tokens are often different.

---

# 📝 Understanding Prompt Fundamentals

I learned that writing an effective prompt requires more than simply asking a question.

A good prompt should include:

- A clear objective
- Proper context
- Specific instructions
- Expected output format
- Any necessary constraints

This understanding will help me write better prompts and receive more accurate responses from AI systems.
