# Day 02 - AI Agents & Large Language Models (LLMs)

## 📖 Overview

On Day 02 of my AI Engineer Journey, I explored one of the most exciting areas of modern Artificial Intelligence: **AI Agents and Large Language Models (LLMs).**

Instead of viewing AI as a simple chatbot, I learned how AI Agents can reason, make decisions, use external tools, and perform tasks autonomously to achieve specific goals.

I also developed a foundational understanding of Large Language Models, their architecture, capabilities, limitations, and their role in powering intelligent AI applications.

# 💡 Key Takeaways

- AI Agents are designed to achieve goals, not just answer questions.
- Large Language Models are the reasoning engine behind many AI applications.
- AI Agents combine reasoning, planning, memory, and tool usage.
- Modern AI systems become more powerful when LLMs are connected with external tools and APIs.
