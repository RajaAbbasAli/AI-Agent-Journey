# 🧠 Understanding Large Language Models

I learned that Large Language Models are trained on massive amounts of text data and generate responses by predicting the next token based on previous context.

Some popular LLMs include:

- GPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Llama
- Mistral

I also learned that LLMs have limitations such as hallucinations, context window limits, and dependence on training data.
