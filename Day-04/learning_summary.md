# 💡 Key Learnings

One of the biggest lessons I learned today is that AI models perform much better when they receive clear and well-structured instructions.

I learned that an effective prompt should include:

- A clear goal
- Relevant context
- Specific instructions
- Defined constraints
- Expected output format

Combining these elements significantly improves the quality and consistency of AI responses.
