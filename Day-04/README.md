# Day 04 - Prompt Engineering Fundamentals

## 📖 Overview

On Day 04 of my AI Engineer Journey, I explored the fundamentals of Prompt Engineering. I learned how to communicate effectively with Large Language Models (LLMs) by writing clear, structured, and goal-oriented prompts.

Prompt Engineering is one of the most valuable skills in modern AI because the quality of AI-generated responses depends heavily on the quality of the prompt provided.

Today, I studied different prompting techniques, prompt structures, and best practices that help AI models produce accurate, relevant, and high-quality outputs.

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of Day 04, I was able to:

- Understand Prompt Engineering.
- Learn why prompts are important.
- Differentiate good prompts from poor prompts.

- Learn the structure of an effective prompt.
