# Day 01 - Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

## 📖 Overview

Day 01 marks the beginning of my AI Engineer Journey. The focus of this day was to build a strong foundation by understanding the core concepts of Artificial Intelligence before moving towards programming and AI Agents.

Rather than jumping directly into coding, I explored the theoretical concepts that explain how AI works, where it is used, and why it is transforming industries around the world.
