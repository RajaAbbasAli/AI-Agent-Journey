# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)

---

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of Computer Science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

These tasks include:

- Learning
- Reasoning
- Problem Solving
- Decision Making
- Language Understanding
- Image Recognition

AI allows computers to analyze data, recognize patterns, and make intelligent decisions.

---

## History of Artificial Intelligence

- 1950 — Alan Turing introduced the idea of machine intelligence.
- 1956 — John McCarthy coined the term "Artificial Intelligence."
- 1997 — IBM Deep Blue defeated the world chess champion.
- 2011 — IBM Watson won the Jeopardy quiz competition.
- Today — AI powers ChatGPT, Self-driving Cars, Recommendation Systems, and many modern applications.

---

# Why is AI Important?

Artificial Intelligence helps automate repetitive tasks, improves decision-making, increases productivity, and solves complex problems.

Benefits include:

- Faster decision making
- Improved accuracy
- Reduced human effort
- Business automation
- Better customer experience
- Large-scale data analysis

# Human Intelligence vs Artificial Intelligence

| Human Intelligence | Artificial Intelligence |
|--------------------|-------------------------|
| Learns from experience | Learns from data |
| Uses emotions | No emotions |
| Creative thinking | Pattern recognition |
| Flexible reasoning | Rule/Data based reasoning |

# Types of Artificial Intelligence

## 1. Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)

- Designed for a specific task.
- Most AI systems today are ANI.

Examples:

- ChatGPT
- Google Assistant
- Face Recognition

---

## 2. Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

- Human-level intelligence.
- Can perform almost every intellectual task.
- Does not yet exist.

---

## 3. Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI)

- Intelligence beyond humans.
- Currently theoretical.

---

# Categories of AI

## Reactive Machines

- No memory.
- Respond only to current input.

Example:

IBM Deep Blue

---

## Limited Memory AI

- Uses previous data to make decisions.

Examples:

- Self-driving Cars
- ChatGPT (within its context window)

---

## Theory of Mind

- Future AI capable of understanding emotions and beliefs.

---

## Self-Aware AI

- AI with consciousness.
- Does not exist today.

---

# Major Branches of AI

## Machine Learning (ML)

Machine Learning allows systems to learn from data without explicit programming.

Examples:

- Spam Detection
- Recommendation Systems
- Fraud Detection

---

## Deep Learning (DL)

Deep Learning uses Artificial Neural Networks to solve complex problems.

Examples:

- Image Recognition
- Speech Recognition
- ChatGPT Models

---

## Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Allows computers to understand and generate human language.

Examples:

- ChatGPT
- Google Translate
- Grammarly

---

## Computer Vision

Allows machines to understand images and videos.

Examples:

- Face Unlock
- Medical Image Analysis
- Object Detection

---

## Robotics

Combines AI with physical machines.

Examples:

- Warehouse Robots
- Industrial Robots
- Surgical Robots

---

## Expert Systems

AI systems designed to imitate human experts.

Examples:

- Medical Diagnosis Systems
- Financial Advisory Systems

---

## Speech Recognition

Converts spoken language into text.

Examples:

- Siri
- Alexa
- Google Assistant

---

# Machine Learning Types

## Supervised Learning

Uses labeled data.

Examples:

- Email Spam Detection
- House Price Prediction

---

## Unsupervised Learning

Uses unlabeled data.

Examples:

- Customer Segmentation
- Data Clustering

---

## Reinforcement Learning

Learns by trial and error using rewards.

Examples:

- Robotics
- Game Playing
- Autonomous Vehicles

---

# Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses Neural Networks with multiple hidden layers.

Applications:

- Image Recognition
- Voice Assistants
- Medical Diagnosis
- Autonomous Vehicles

# Interview Questions

### What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence by machines to perform tasks such as learning, reasoning, and decision-making.

### What is the difference between AI, ML, and DL?

- AI is the broad field.
- ML is a subset of AI.
- DL is a subset of Machine Learning.

### What are the three types of AI?

- Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)
- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
- Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI)

### Why is AI important?

AI improves efficiency, automates repetitive tasks, enhances decision-making, and enables intelligent applications.
