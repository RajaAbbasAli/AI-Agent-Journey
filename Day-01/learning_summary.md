# 💡 Key Learnings

Today I understood that Artificial Intelligence is much more than chatbots. It is a broad field of computer science that enables machines to learn from data, recognize patterns, make decisions, and solve problems that normally require human intelligence.

I also learned that:

- Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence.
- Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning.
- Modern AI systems rely heavily on data rather than fixed programming rules.
- AI is already being used in healthcare, education, finance, transportation, cybersecurity, and many other industries.
